# NGC 2207 and IC 2163, reproduced

Rank 9 of [twenty galaxies to reproduce](twenty-galaxies.md), done the way [the
Cartwheel](cartwheel.md) was: a published model, grids of previews scored against the picture, the
best run at 8 M, the freeze where the take looks like the picture with a split screen, then one
continuous flight through both galaxies. Everything is in `references/ngc2207/`.

## What is known of the pair

Struck et al. 2005 (MNRAS 364, 69), the hydrodynamical model after Elmegreen et al. 1995:

- 35 Mpc (1 arcsec = 170 pc). The nuclei 85 arcsec apart on the sky, 14.5 kpc. NGC 2207
  inclined about 35 degrees, IC 2163 about 40.
- Halo masses inside the primary's disk radius 1.5 and 1.1 x 10^11 solar masses, a ratio of
  about 0.73; rotation peaking at 160 to 170 and 140 km/s; stellar disks of 9 and 4.8 kpc, gas
  disks of 19.6 and 6.8.
- A first passage on a bound, nearly circular orbit, IC 2163 sweeping past the west side of
  NGC 2207 about 22 kpc out and on round to the east, about 280 Myr ago. Prograde for IC 2163,
  which the long tide pulls into its eye-shaped oval and a tail; retrograde for NGC 2207, whose
  arms the encounter leaves alone. IC 2163 is behind. The orbit is seen nearly face on.

## The picture

`heic2414a`, Webb (MIRI) and Hubble, 6353 x 2983 px, CC BY 4.0 (NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI). Its
288.8 arcsec are 49.0 kpc at 35 Mpc, so `match --kpc-across 49.0`, and the two nuclei are marked
on the map (NGC 2207 at 0.657, 0.241 of the width, IC 2163 at 0.370, 0.221). Measured in linear
light, three quarters of the bodies are red, (1, 0.34, 0.27), Hubble's blue clusters 2 % of
them, NGC 2207's core a pale cream; the photo ruler reads lit 0.585, L50 0.419, L99 0.839,
saturated 0.562 on it.

## The scene

`tools/make_scene.py` writes it from the two spirals of scene 23 of batch 20261121 (an Antennae
draw), resized after the model and the picture: NGC 2207 a Hernquist halo of 3 x 10^11 solar
masses and 10 kpc, its disk 5 kpc in scale length cut at four; IC 2163 that mass times the
ratio, the scale times its square root, its disk 3.2 kpc cut at 3.8. NGC 2207 retrograde to the
orbit, IC 2163 prograde, the pericentre asked at 260 Myr so the film has an approach.

- **The disks are the picture's, not the model's stars.** Drawn at the stellar sizes (3.6 and
  2.3 kpc in scale length) the bodies came out half the picture's size and every match took the
  first frame. Webb sees the dust out to Struck's gas disk, 20 kpc from NGC 2207's centre.
- **A scene written by hand is placed only when its solver changes.** `scene.json` says
  `restricted` and explore runs `--solver barnes-hut`, so the encounter is placed again with the
  live halos' centre model; written `barnesHut`, the galaxies keep the template's start.
- Live halos drag: the pericentre asked at 16 kpc lands at 13.7, 8 Myr late.

## Two grids

Each scene explored at 200 k, run at 500 k (`--sticky-gas on`) and matched, about 2.2 minutes.

1. **Mass ratio x pericentre x eccentricity** (0.55, 0.75, 0.95 x 16, 22, 28 kpc x 0.3, 0.6,
   0.9), 27 scenes in an hour. The lighter companion, the closer passage and the weakly bound
   orbit win: 0.628, 21 Myr after the passage.
2. **The disks' orientations** round it: NGC 2207's inclination to the orbit (0.25, 0.5, 0.85
   rad) x IC 2163's (0.35, 0.7, 1.05) x IC 2163's line of nodes (0, 2.09, 4.19), the bulges raised
   to 0.14 and 0.10 for the picture's cores. 0.633 for 0.85, 1.05 and 4.19: IC 2163 drawn out to
   the left into an oval with its tail, as in the picture, and the best of the four scenes of the
   54 whose free match also chooses a moment after the passage.

The sheets are `sheet-grid1.jpg` and `sheet-grid2.jpg` beside the table, `sweep.csv`.

**Plain disks score well.** Over the whole take all but those four match best before the
passage (0.66 to 0.71): two intact disks overlapping fit the bodies, which the isophotes
weigh most. `tools/match.sh` therefore searches from 20 to 460 Myr after the run's own first
pericentre (`peri.py`), where the model puts the picture. Scores of one scene redrawn with only its
bulges changed moved by 0.04, so a grid separates families, not neighbours; the sheets decided.

**The moment is earlier than the model's.** The match lands 24 Myr after the passage, against
Struck's 280. His halos are rigid and keep IC 2163 circling at 20 kpc; live ones drain the bound
orbit (out to 28 kpc 120 Myr after the passage, back and merged by 800), and the eye is sharpest
just after the passage. The restricted solver was tried and left: its centres are point masses
of the whole galaxy, which put the same orbit 88 kpc out.

## The 8 M take

8 M visible and 12 M of halo, sticky gas, 148 to 658 Myr: 4 354 s and a 10.6 GB take. The wide
disks' softening gives a step of 0.072 Myr, twice the Cartwheel's, hence half its time. The
match on it: 0.636 at 291.6 Myr, 24 Myr after the pericentre, elevation 11.9, azimuth 28.7, roll
-170 (`match.json`).

## Webb's colours

The Cartwheel's `dustGlow` draws each grain into the light; at 8 M it made broad salmon ribbons
where Webb shows thin red veins. **The glow now follows the dust web**: a look with `dustWeb`
draws the painted strands' grains into the light as before and, in the pass that multiplies the
carried dust by the web, adds the web's dust times the glow's colour, so the light comes out in
the web's filaments (`webGlowFragment`). Before, a frame with a web drew no glow at all. Look
`ngc2207-webb.look.json`: web 0.8, glow 0.3 in (1, 0.26, 0.24), brightness 0.25, stretch 1,
white point 2.6, saturation 1.8, dust 0.6, cores cream for NGC 2207 and yellow for IC 2163,
young stars pink-cream, knots blue. The ruler reads L50 0.53, L99 0.88, saturated 0.86.

What is still missing: Hubble's blue clusters (the knots are too few to show), and the arms are
wider and brighter than the picture's, which leaves less dark between them.

## The flight and the montage

`tools/flights.py` writes one continuous flight of 206 s, after the Cartwheel's `film3`: the
approach seen from the side 110 Myr before the passage, the camera closing while time eases
into a freeze on the picture's view at 30 s; nine still seconds; into IC 2163's eye face on and
through its disk; across to NGC 2207 with time frozen, down its axis and out under it, along its
plane through the arms, a turn low over them; then a pull back while time runs to the end of the
take, IC 2163 coming round again. `inside` lighter than the Cartwheel's: stars as points 6 to
14 kpc out, 24 a particle, 2 renders a frame at most, glow haze 5 and fade 0.5.

`tools/compose.py`: over the hold the frame shrinks into the left half and the picture slides in
on the right, cropped to its band, with the title and the credit. The picture is 2.13:1, too
wide for the Cartwheel's half-frame split.

Costs: the 54 previews, two hours; the 8 M hires, 1.2 h; the 4K ProRes flight, 0.9 h for 6 181
frames. At the Cartwheel's inside (128 stars a particle, up to 8 renders a frame, 8 to 20 kpc)
the wide disks put 128 M point stars in a render and an image inside them took 17 s, 45 times
the lighter settings, for no difference visible on the same frames.
