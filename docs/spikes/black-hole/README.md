# A black hole close up: the spike's pictures (#314)

What [docs/notes/black-hole-close-up.md](https://github.com/pierregc/sillage/blob/main/docs/notes/black-hole-close-up.md)
shows, from one take of `scene.json`: six million tracers, a frame every 250 time units.

- `tear.jpg`: the first return (t 729) and the settled disk (t 2979), from above drawn straight,
  and from 30° with the light bent; coloured by the run's heat.
- `temperature.jpg`: the same frames from above, the temperature from the radius against from
  the crossing.
- `lensing.jpg`: the settled disk from 80°, 60° and 10°, straight against bent; temperature from
  the radius, 6500 K at the hottest.
- `looks.jpg`: at 10°, without and with beaming, with the photon ring, at 11 000 K; and the
  shadow close up, two images against three.
- `orbit.mp4` and `orbit-tiles.jpg`: ten seconds round the settled disk at 10°, the run going on,
  the light bent, the temperature from the crossing.
- `story.mp4`: the whole run from 25° and 75 r_s, exposure and temperatures held at the settled
  frame's.
