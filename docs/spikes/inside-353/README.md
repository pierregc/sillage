# Two disks that cross (#353)

The sheets of `docs/notes/inside-a-galaxy.md`, "Two disks that cross", drawn from the Mice take
(2026-11-04 scene-0067, 8 M) with `export --flight <name> --flight-keys <dir> --flight-at <s,...>`.

- `cause-550.jpg`, `cause-440.jpg`: a frame of #297's flight as drawn, without dust, as plain
  points by galaxy and the larger galaxy alone (`plain-rb.look.json`, `plain-g0.look.json`).
- `history.jpg`: the matter from 90 kpc above the larger disk, 420 to 452 Myr (`history.flight.json`).
- `freeze-452-430.jpg`: frames 440 to 580 frozen at 452 and at 430 Myr (`dive-430.flight.json`).
- `crossing-430.jpg`, `dust-430.jpg`: frame 550 frozen at 430 Myr, and its dust's optical depth.
- `matter.py`, `kick.py`: the take's positions read without the renderer.
